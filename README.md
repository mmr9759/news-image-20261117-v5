## The image

A front page, **"THE WORLD TODAY"**, drawn programmatically with Node 22 (stdlib only, hand-rolled PNG encoder + 5×7 bitmap font) from real headlines of the day — no AI image generator, so every word is sharp and legible:

- **Masthead + date:** Tuesday 17 November 2026 — 40 stories, 6 continents.
- **Lead story:** Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge (2nd attack in 2 days) + Russia's reply on Kyiv's Dnipro bridge — illustrated with a broken bridge, smoke and explosion.
- **3 majors:** 4-nation Gaza aid push, Trump–Xi–Putin / APEC summit, UK sanctions on 8 settlement firms.
- **Map:** world map with 13 numbered pins.
- **Grid:** 36 stories across six continents + "The Day in Numbers" column and figures strip.

## Run it

node main.ts
